# ST8WRX Brand System

## Name

**ST8WRX** is the public master brand.

Pronunciation is intentionally natural rather than prescriptive. The name carries the ideas of **state**, **work**, **execution**, and **systems that become real** without presenting as a crypto-native brand.

Do not expand the name to "StateWorks" in product UI, repository titles, or public-facing copy. "State works" may be used as an occasional verbal motif, not as the company or product name.

## Positioning

ST8WRX is an AI-native venture creation network where humans, agents, compute, IP, and capital can assemble around projects, build together, prove contribution, and launch or transact around what they create.

Bitcoin SV is infrastructure beneath the product, not the front-door identity.

## Product hierarchy

- **ST8WRX** — platform and master brand
- **ST8 Projects** — venture/project workspace
- **ST8 Agents** — agent identities, teams, execution, provenance
- **ST8 Compute** — shared compute and metering
- **ST8 Ledger** — contribution and provenance state
- **ST8 Market** — projects, products, compute, agents, licenses, bounties
- **ST8 Launch** — project economy and asset launch infrastructure

Names are working product architecture labels until dedicated UX naming is finalized.

## Visual direction

The core visual language is precise, minimal, technical, and quiet.

- dark neutral foundation rather than pure black
- high-contrast off-white typography
- geometric systems imagery
- node, graph, state-transition, and assembly metaphors
- restrained motion
- no blockchain coins, chains, hexagons, rockets, cyberpunk neon, or generic AI-brain imagery

### Core colors

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ink-950` | `#0B0D12` | primary dark surface |
| `paper-50` | `#F4F7FB` | primary light / mark |
| `slate-500` | `#747B89` | secondary copy |
| `signal` | product-defined | limited interactive accent; do not bake into the core mark |

The primary mark is intentionally monochrome so product themes and future token/economic surfaces can introduce accent color without fragmenting the identity.

## Mark

`docs/assets/st8wrx-mark.svg`

The mark represents connected state transitions through eight nodes. It should be treated as a system mark, not a literal S, 8, blockchain, or network diagram.

Use the supplied vector. Do not redraw individual nodes, change their count, stretch the geometry, or add effects to the master artwork.

## Wordmark

`docs/assets/st8wrx-wordmark.svg`

Use uppercase `ST8WRX` in product chrome, repository identity, launch material, and formal references.

Avoid `St8wrx`, `ST8 Wrks`, `StateWorks`, `ST8Works`, and similar variants as brand names.

## Voice

ST8WRX should speak like infrastructure for builders, not a token promoter.

Prefer:

- Build together.
- Prove what you created.
- Bring your agents. Bring your compute.
- Work has receipts.
- From idea to operating system.

Avoid:

- moon / pump / alpha language
- "revolutionary blockchain"
- "decentralized everything"
- financial-return promises
- explaining BSV before explaining the user problem
